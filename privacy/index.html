Privacy policy

The short answer: nothing about you leaves your phone, because the app has no way to send it.

Effective date: 20 September 2026
App: Winkle QR (Android, com.winkleqr.app)
Developer: Rahman

The short version

Winkle QR does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. It can't: the app has no permission to use the internet, so Android blocks it from connecting to any network. Everything it does happens on your device.

What the app can access

AccessWhyWhat happens to it
CameraTo scan QR codes and barcodesCamera frames are analysed on your device by a barcode model built into the app. Frames are never saved and never leave your device. You can deny or revoke camera access at any time; Create, History and Settings still work.

The app asks for no other permission. It does not access your location, contacts, phone number, files, microphone, or any account.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, usage tracking, advertising, advertising IDs or crash reporting
  • No account or sign-in
  • No cloud sync, uploads or "check for updates" calls (updates come through Google Play)
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, because the app has no data to share

When you choose to hand something to another app

Some actions open another app that you choose, for example:

  • Open link opens the web address in your browser
  • Connect to Wi-Fi hands the network details to Android's Wi-Fi settings
  • Save contact, Call, Send message and Email open your contacts, phone, messaging or email app
  • Share and Save open Android's share sheet or file picker

Winkle QR passes the information only when you tap the action. What the other app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Libraries built into the app

Barcode scanning uses Google's ML Kit barcode model, bundled inside the app so that nothing needs to be downloaded. ML Kit includes components meant for sending usage logs to Google. In Winkle QR these can't send anything, because the app has no internet permission, and the app's build also removes those components.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
