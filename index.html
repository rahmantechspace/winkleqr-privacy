What it does

Everything clever happens on your phone.

The barcode model ships inside the app, so nothing is uploaded to be recognised. Not once.

📷

Scan anything, fast

QR codes and everyday barcodes (EAN, UPC, Code 128, Code 39) with flashlight, pinch zoom, tap to focus and a highlight on the code it locked onto. Got a code in a screenshot instead? Pick the photo and it reads that too, even if you have refused camera access.

🔗

It knows what it found

Links, Wi-Fi, contacts, phone numbers, SMS, email, products. Each gets the right action, and links show the real domain so a lookalike stands out before you tap.

Proof, not vibes

How the claim is kept honest.

Anyone can write "we respect your privacy". Here is what actually stands behind it.

  • No internet permissionAndroid grants network access through a permission this app never requests, so it is blocked by the operating system rather than by our good intentions.
  • The build refuses to ship otherwiseEvery build inspects the finished app and fails if any permission beyond the camera appears, including ones a third party library tries to slip in.
  • The scanner lives inside the appThe barcode model is bundled, so nothing is downloaded and no image is sent away to be recognised.
  • History is excluded from backupsIt is not copied to Google Drive and does not follow you to a new phone.
  • Hand offs are yours to makeOpening a link, joining Wi-Fi or saving a contact passes the details to an app you already trust, only when you tap the button.
  • 601 automated testsRun on Android 7 through 17, on phones, foldables and tablets, before anything ships.
