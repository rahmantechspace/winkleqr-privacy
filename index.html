This one removed the option.

A QR code can hold your Wi-Fi password, your contact card, a payment link. Winkle QR doesn't hold the INTERNET permission, so the operating system blocks it from opening any connection — and the build itself fails if that ever changes. Check it yourself: Settings → Apps → Winkle QR → Permissions.

What it does

Everything clever happens
on your phone.

The barcode model ships inside the app, so nothing is uploaded for recognition — not even once.

📷

Scan anything, fast

QR codes and the everyday barcodes — EAN, UPC, Code 128, Code 39 — with flashlight, pinch zoom, tap to focus and a highlight on the code it locked onto. Got a code in a screenshot instead? Pick the photo and it reads that too, even if you've refused camera access.

🔗

It knows what it found

Links, Wi-Fi, contacts, phone numbers, SMS, email, products — each gets the right action. Links show the real domain, so a lookalike stands out before you tap.

🎨

Design the code

Colours and gradients, dot and eye shapes, a logo in the middle, frames — and any colour you like, not just presets.

✅

It scans itself

Before you save, the app reads its own design back. If a colour choice would break it, you're told straight away instead of finding out on a printed sign.

🖨️

13 original designs, exported as an image or a sharp vector PDF in A4, Letter or A5.

🕓

History that stays home

Saved on the phone only, and left out of cloud backups and device transfers. Delete one, or clear the lot.

Your codes and posters come out clean. No logo of ours, no "upgrade to remove", no subscription.

Proof, not vibes

How the claim is kept honest.

Anyone can write "we respect your privacy". Here's what actually stands behind it.

  • No internet permissionAndroid grants network access through a permission this app never requests, so it's blocked at the operating-system level — not by our good intentions.
  • The build refuses to ship otherwiseEvery build inspects the finished app and fails if any permission beyond the camera appears, including ones a third-party library tries to slip in.
  • The scanner lives inside the appThe barcode model is bundled, so nothing is downloaded and no image is sent away to be recognised.
  • Your history is excluded from backupsIt isn't copied to Google Drive and doesn't follow you to a new phone unless you choose to recreate it.
  • Hand-offs are yours to makeOpening a link, joining Wi-Fi or saving a contact passes details to an app you already trust, only when you tap the button.
  • 601 automated testsRun on Android 7 through 17, on phones, foldables and tablets, before anything ships.
